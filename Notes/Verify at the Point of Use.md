# Verify at the Point of Use

Don't rely on a check you didn't witness. Whoever is about to act verifies
the credential itself, at that moment, rather than assuming an earlier
check elsewhere was done.

Gate staff at a festival check the wristband itself even though a different
team issued it hours ago: is it genuine, today's, and for this area. The
related security term is "never trust, always verify".

Different from [[Proof of Possession]]: that is about holding something
only the real party has, this is about where the checking happens.
