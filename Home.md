# Home

Entry point into the vault. Start here, follow links out to topic MOCs.

## Commands

- **Capture** - drop a rough note/question straight into `Fleeting/`, zero
  effort. No command needed.
- **`/learning-note-formatting`** - tidy up what's piled up in `Fleeting/`:
  frontmatter, dedup, links, files it into the right MOC. Mechanical only,
  doesn't teach anything.
- **`/learning-foundations`** - an actual learning session. Scans for gaps,
  or point it straight at a fleeting capture/backlog line/named topic.
  What → why → how, quizzed and graded, only writes once you can explain
  it back in your own words.
- **Recall** - just ask directly ("what do I know about X") - no command,
  reads the vault since it's plain markdown.
- **Commit** - after a formatting or foundations session.

See `README.md` for the full walkthrough.
